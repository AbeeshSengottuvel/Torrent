Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, honest answers about Haven's security, architecture, and permissions.

No. Haven is engineered with least-privilege security principles. The Windows installer installs strictly into %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Haven and does not write to system-wide protected directories or require UAC elevation.

Microsoft Defender SmartScreen assigns trust based on historical download volume and costly commercial EV certificates. As a new, non-commercial open-source release, Haven does not pay annual certificate fees. Simply click "More info" and "Run anyway", or use Haven-Setup.bat to install via native PowerShell.

Unlike standard clients that use software-level toggles, Haven binds peer sockets directly to your selected VPN network adapter interface (e.g. WireGuard wg0 or OpenVPN tun0). If the VPN drops, the kernel rejects outgoing packets immediately within 1 second. It never falls back to an unprotected connection.

Downloaded payloads are written exclusively to your selected download folder (e.g. C:\Downloads). Settings and state are stored locally in an isolated SQLite database in %APPDATA%\Haven. Haven never transmits your files, history, or IP addresses anywhere.

Haven's uninstaller strictly removes application binaries and shortcuts. Under Security Invariant INV-03, the uninstaller is cryptographically blocked from ever modifying, moving, or deleting user download directories.
